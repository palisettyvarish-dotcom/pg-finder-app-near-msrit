COMMON STUDENT QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from incoming 1st year and senior MSRITians.

What are the curfew and gate timings for PGs near MSRIT?
Most girls PGs near MSRIT (such as Serenity Elite and Sri Durga) have biometric closing hours between 10:00 PM and 10:30 PM for safety. Boys and unisex coliving residences (like Stanza Living Poznan and CoHo) offer 24/7 biometric smart-lock access or flexible extension up to 11:30 PM with college ID for project and lab work.
How far are these PGs from the MSRIT classroom blocks?
All listed properties are within 100 meters to 700 meters of MSRIT Gate 1 (Mathikere), Gate 2 (Main Administrative block), or Gate 10 (Sports complex). You can comfortably walk to class in 2 to 6 minutes, eliminating Bangalore traffic delays.
Is food provided on weekends and exam days?
Yes! All full-service PGs provide breakfast, lunch, and dinner on both Saturdays and Sundays, often featuring special South/North Indian feasts and biryani. Packed lunch boxes are also provided if you have continuous lab schedules inside MSRIT.
Do these PGs charge any brokerage or hidden deposit deductions?
Zero brokerage! When you connect through findmyhome, you deal directly with the property management. The standard security deposit is 1 month's rent (refundable with 30 days departure notice).
What happens during Bangalore BESCOM power outages?
All featured residences in our catalog have automatic diesel generator power backup that powers bedroom lights, ceiling fans, and high-speed fiber internet routers uninterrupted during study hours.
